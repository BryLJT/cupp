Type direction

A warm serif for bean names, a quiet sans for everything else.

Proposal: Fraunces (display — it's practically the house face of specialty coffee) for the wordmark, bean names, and scores; Inter for UI, labels, and body. Both ship via @expo-google-fonts on SDK 54. The database's alternative pairing was Calistoga + Inter if Fraunces feels too editorial. Decide when we build the theme file — nothing above depends on it.

Scope flag & open questions

⚠ Likes & comments are on the cut list

Handover §9 cuts all social features — follows, likes, comments, community feeds — agreed 16 Jul. What's in scope is the private journal: your own logs, newest first. These wireframes keep Instagram's photo-forward card anatomy, so if the team un-cuts social post-hackathon, a like/comment row slots into the card footer without relayout. Building it now needs a group-chat decision with Bryan first, per §13.

  1. Is “feed” meant as your own history, or seeing other people's logs?The wireframes assume private journal (in scope). If you meant a shared feed, that's a §9 conversation before any code.
  2. Bottom bar: are Journal / ◉ Scan / ＋ New log the right three?Alternative: drop ＋ from the bar and offer “type it in manually” only inside the scan flow. Keeping both makes manual entry one tap — safer for the demo if Wi-Fi or Agnes misbehaves.
  3. Score scale — 0–10 with one decimal (8.7), or something coarser?Decimal reads “specialty” (cupping scores); a 5-star reads consumer. Affects the form, the card badge, and seed data.
  4. Where does sign-out live?Wireframe puts an avatar in the Journal header → tiny account sheet. Cheaper than a fourth tab.
